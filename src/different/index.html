Six areas

Six areas, one Readiness Check

AgentiCCells's summaryTheir home page lists six areas of combined expertise. The names below are theirs, as published. The line under each is our plain-language summary of what the field usually covers, not their words.1

“Most AI governance solutions focus on only one of these dimensions: Legal compliance, technical controls, or policy.”1

What each area covers

AgentiCCells's plain-language summary

  1. AI governance: Who decides how AI is used, who is accountable, and how decisions are recorded.
  2. Regulatory and legal risk: Which laws and rules apply to the way an organisation builds, buys or uses AI.
  3. AI assurance and audit readiness: Being able to show evidence that controls exist and work, when someone asks.
  4. Human rights and ethical safeguards: Protecting the people affected by AI decisions: fairness, privacy, dignity and redress.
  5. Public policy and emerging regulation: Knowing what is changing, so today's choices still hold next year.
  6. SME operational realities: Advice sized for small teams with limited time and budget, not for large compliance departments.
