For ecosystem partners

Your clients are asking about AI. Give them one consistent answer.

SUSTAIN-NOBILITY's site describes a second audience: advisers, law firms and SME consultancies that support many SMEs and want one scalable tool to do it.

Who this is for

Advisers

Business and risk advisers whose SME clients use AI and need a starting point for governance.

Law firms

Firms whose SME clients ask what AI regulation means for them.

SME consultancies

Consultancies that work with many small organisations at once.

Why a shared tool

When many clients ask the same question, a shared, structured check lets a partner give each one the same assessment, the same governance score and the same kind of output, rather than starting from a blank page each time.

The paragraph above is AgentiCCells's summary of the partner case on SUSTAIN-NOBILITY's site ("one scalable tool" for partners supporting many SMEs). It makes no claim about existing partners, of which this proposal names none.
