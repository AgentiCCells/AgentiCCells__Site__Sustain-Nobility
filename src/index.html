Six areas, one check

Their site says:

“Most AI governance solutions focus on only one of these dimensions: Legal compliance, technical controls, or policy.”1

The six areas as SUSTAIN-NOBILITY's home page lists them; the line under each is AgentiCCells's plain-language summary. No area is highlighted.

The six areas

  1. 01

    AI governance

    Who decides how AI is used, who is accountable, and how decisions are recorded.

  2. 02

    Regulatory and legal risk

    Which laws and rules apply to the way an organisation builds, buys or uses AI.

  3. 03

    AI assurance and audit readiness

    Being able to show evidence that controls exist and work, when someone asks.

  4. 04

    Human rights and ethical safeguards

    Protecting the people affected by AI decisions: fairness, privacy, dignity and redress.

  5. 05

    Public policy and emerging regulation

    Knowing what is changing, so today's choices still hold next year.

  6. 06

    SME operational realities

    Advice sized for small teams with limited time and budget, not for large compliance departments.

Proposal

Suggestions from AgentiCCells for SUSTAIN-NOBILITY to consider. These are our proposals, not their claims.

  • Proposal: Lead with the question the site already owns, "Can you prove it is governed responsibly?", and make evidence you can show the thread through every page.
  • Proposal: Use one product name everywhere. Their site uses both Readiness Check and Health Check.
  • Proposal: Give SMEs and advisers two clear paths from the home page, with Partner Licensing as the adviser offer.
  • Proposal: Name the frameworks the check maps to, once the company confirms the mapping, in place of 'the main UK and EU regulators'.
  • Proposal: Add a short, dated panel on what changed this year in EU and UK rules, with the date it was last checked.
  • Proposal: Say plainly what each plan delivers and whether prices will be published.
  • Proposal: Carry the Disclaimer's plain words into the main copy: the check is indicative, not legal advice or certification.
  • Proposal: Correct small wording slips on the current site, such as 'assess responsibly governance maturity' and 'concommitantly', and complete the sentence about the real-time diagnostic.
  • Proposal: Publish an accessibility statement and move the Terms of Use to their own address.
