How it works

From a 10-minute assessment to a board-ready report

The AI Governance Readiness Check is built to produce practical, defensible outputs in days instead of months, and to run again when your strategy changes.

The steps in words

  1. Assessment. A 10-minute diagnostic of how AI is used and governed in the organisation.
  2. Governance score. A measure of governance maturity, so you can see where you stand.
  3. Executive snapshot. An initial snapshot of the results, written for executives.
  4. Report. In the Professional plan, a 30-minute review call and an initial executive board report.
  5. Monitoring. Real-time diagnostics when strategy changes, so governance keeps pace with how AI is used.

Step descriptions follow what sustain-nobility.com published on 2026-11-18. The questions in the assessment and the scoring method are not published there, so this page does not describe them.
