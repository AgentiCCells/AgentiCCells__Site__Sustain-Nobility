How the Readiness Check works

From a 10-minute assessment to a board report1

AgentiCCells's summaryFive steps, drawn from what their home page lists in the plans and the hero. The step names are ours; the published wording is quoted under each step.

The steps in words

  1. Assessment

    Published wording: “10-minute Assessment”1

    AgentiCCells's summaryA short diagnostic of how AI is used and governed in the organisation.

  2. Score

    Published wording: “Governance Score”1

    AgentiCCells's summaryA score of governance maturity, so the organisation can see where it stands.

  3. Snapshot

    Published wording: “Initial Executive Snapshot Summary”1

    AgentiCCells's summaryA first summary of the results, written for executives.

  4. Report

    Published wording: “30-min review call; Initial Executive Board Report”1

    AgentiCCells's summaryIn the Professional plan, a review call and a first report for the board.

  5. Monitoring

    Published wording: “Additionally, receive a diagnostic in real time when the company changes its strategy, it can be alerted.”1

    AgentiCCells's summaryTheir site describes a diagnostic in real time when the company changes its strategy. The name 'Monitoring' is ours.

Step wording follows what sustain-nobility.com published. The assessment questions and the scoring method are not published there, so this page does not describe them.
