## Home

> If your client's AI caused harm tomorrow, who would be accountable?

The question is no longer "Are you using AI?" The question is "Can you prove it is governed responsibly?"

The AI Governance Readiness Check helps organisations assess their AI governance maturity, identify risks, and produce practical, defensible outputs in days instead of months, with real-time diagnostics when strategy changes.

Who it is for:

- **SMEs** that use AI but are unsure about the risks.
- **Ecosystem partners**: advisers, law firms and SME consultancies supporting many SMEs with one scalable tool.

Start the readiness check or book a strategy call at https://www.sustain-nobility.com

## How it works

1. **Assessment**: a 10-minute diagnostic.
2. **Governance score**: governance maturity.
3. **Executive snapshot**: initial, for executives.
4. **Report** (Professional): a 30-minute review call and an initial executive board report.
5. **Monitoring**: real-time diagnostics when strategy changes. Their site does not say which plan includes this.

## What makes it different

Six areas of expertise in one check (names as SUSTAIN-NOBILITY lists them):

## Context, with sources

AgentiCCells research, not legal advice; not re-checked against the sources on 2026-11-18.

- EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689: in force 1 August 2024; applies in stages from 2 February 2025 to 2 August 2027 (Article 113). Can reach providers and deployers outside the EU where output is used in the EU (Article 2). Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj
- In November 2025 the European Commission proposed changes to the high-risk timetable ("digital omnibus"); status to be checked. Source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai
- ISO/IEC 42001:2023, AI management systems, published December 2023, certifiable. Source: https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html
- UK white paper "A pro-innovation approach to AI regulation", March 2023: five principles applied by existing regulators; government response February 2024. Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-regulation-a-pro-innovation-approach
- ICO guidance on AI and data protection. Source: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/
- NIST AI RMF 1.0, January 2023: govern, map, measure, manage. Source: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
