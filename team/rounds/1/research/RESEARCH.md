## The field: EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, UK approach, NIST

- **f1** The EU AI Act is Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, done at Brussels on 13 June 2024. It entered into force on the twentieth day after publication (1 August 2024) and applies generally from 2 August 2026. Quote: "It shall apply from 2 August 2026." ([source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32024R1689), quoted, read 2026-11-18)
- **f2** Original staged dates in Article 113: Chapters I and II (general provisions, AI literacy, prohibited practices) from 2 February 2025; notified bodies, general-purpose AI models, governance and penalties (Chapter III Section 4, Chapters V, VII, XII, Article 78, except Article 101) from 2 August 2025; Article 6(1) from 2 August 2027. ([source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32024R1689), paraphrased, read 2026-11-18)
- **f3** The Commission's AI Act page gives the timeline: entry into force 1 August 2024; prohibitions and AI literacy from 2 February 2025; GPAI and governance rules from 2 August 2025; general application 2 August 2026 with exceptions (page last updated 19 October 2026). ([source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai), paraphrased, read 2026-11-18)
- **f4** The Commission proposed the AI Omnibus (Digital Omnibus on AI, COM(2025) 836) on 19 November 2025 as part of its digital simplification package; a political agreement was reached on 7 May 2026, and the Omnibus entered into force on 27 July 2026. ([source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai), paraphrased, read 2026-11-18)
- **f5** The Omnibus was adopted as Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 of 8 July 2026, amending Regulations (EU) 2024/1689, 2018/1139 and 2023/1230, and entered into force on the third day after its publication in the Official Journal. Quote: "as regards the simplification of the implementation of harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (Digital Omnibus on AI)" ([source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202601744), quoted, read 2026-11-18)
- **f6** Regulation 2026/1744 amends Article 113: high-risk rules in Chapter III Sections 1 to 3 now apply from 2 December 2027 for Annex III (stand-alone) systems and from 2 August 2028 for Annex I (product-embedded) systems. Quote: "2 December 2027 as regards AI systems classified as high-risk pursuant to Article 6(2) and Annex III" ([source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202601744), quoted, read 2026-11-18)
- **f7** Regulation 2026/1744 adds new prohibitions to Article 5 (AI systems generating non-consensual intimate material and child sexual abuse material) applying from 2 December 2026, and gives providers of generative systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 until 2 December 2026 to meet Article 50(2) marking duties. ([source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202601744), paraphrased, read 2026-11-18)
- **f8** The original Article 4 required providers and deployers to ensure, to their best extent, a sufficient level of AI literacy of staff, applying from 2 February 2025. Quote: "shall take measures to ensure, to their best extent, a sufficient level of AI literacy of their staff" ([source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32024R1689), quoted, read 2026-11-18)
- **f9** Regulation 2026/1744 replaces Article 4: providers and deployers must take measures to support AI literacy, without having to guarantee any specific level; the Commission and Member States must support them, in particular SMEs. Quote: "This obligation does not require providers or deployers to guarantee any specific level of AI literacy of any individual." ([source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202601744), quoted, read 2026-11-18)
- **f10** Recital 8 of Regulation 2026/1744 says stringent AI literacy obligations created an additional compliance burden, particularly for smaller enterprises. ([source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202601744), paraphrased, read 2026-11-18)
